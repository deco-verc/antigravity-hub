# Brutalist & Industrial Design System

## 1. Palette & Surfaces
- **Canvas:** `#000000` / `#ffffff`
- **Contrast Surfaces:** `#18181b` / `#f4f4f5`
- **Border:** `2px solid #27272a` (Dark) / `2px solid #000000` (Light)
- **Accent:** Electric Amber `#f59e0b` or Acid Lime `#84cc16`
- **Text:** `#ffffff` / `#000000`

## 2. Typography
- **Headings:** Bold geometric or heavy grotesk (uppercase tags, tabular data, sharp contrast).
- **Code & Metadata:** Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`, `Geist Mono`, `monospace`).
- **Numbers/Metrics:** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` mandatory.

## 3. Interaction
- **Dials:** `VARIANCE: 9 | MOTION: 2 | DENSITY: 7`
- **Transitions:** Sharp snap transitions (100ms linear or instant), raw hover inversion.
